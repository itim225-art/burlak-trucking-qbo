Disconnect from QuickBooks Online

Burlak Trucking QuickBooks Integration · Private internal application

Authorization for this application can be revoked at any time. Because this is a private internal tool with no public accounts, disconnecting is done directly in QuickBooks Online.

How to disconnect

  1. Sign in to QuickBooks Online as an administrator of the company.
  2. Go to Settings → Manage apps (or Apps → My apps).
  3. Find this application in the connected apps list.
  4. Select Disconnect and confirm.

What happens when you disconnect

  • The application immediately loses all ability to read data from the QuickBooks Online company. Its stored authorization token stops working.
  • The locally stored token is deleted from the company-controlled machine.
  • No data is retained by the application after disconnection. The application maintains no external database or cloud copy of QuickBooks data.
  • Nothing in your QuickBooks Online company is altered or removed by disconnecting.

Authorization also lapses on its own if the connection goes unused past Intuit's refresh-token expiry window.
