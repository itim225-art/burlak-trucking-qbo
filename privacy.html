Privacy Policy

Burlak Trucking QuickBooks Integration · Last updated: August 15, 2026

1. Who we are

This application ("the Application") is operated by Burlak Trucking Inc., a Florida corporation (USDOT 3277757, MC 1329831) based in Jacksonville, Florida. Contact: buinetta.trucking@gmail.com.

2. Scope — this is a private, internal application

The Application is used solely by Burlak Trucking Inc. to access its own QuickBooks Online accounting records. It is not distributed, sold, licensed, or made available to any other business or individual. It has no customers, no public users, and no account registration. It does not collect information from members of the public.

3. What data the Application accesses

When authorized by a company owner, the Application reads accounting data belonging to Burlak Trucking Inc. from QuickBooks Online, which may include:

  • Chart of accounts, general ledger entries, and trial balances
  • Transactions: expenses, purchases, deposits, transfers, bills, and payments
  • Vendor, customer, and employee records associated with the company file
  • Financial reports, including profit and loss, balance sheet, and cash flow

The Application is currently configured for read-only access. It does not create, modify, or delete records in QuickBooks Online.

4. How the data is used

Accounting data is used only for the company's own internal bookkeeping, expense categorization, financial review, and tax preparation. It is never used for advertising, never sold, and never shared with data brokers or marketing services.

5. Where the data goes

  • Local machine. The Application runs on a computer controlled by Burlak Trucking Inc. The OAuth refresh token that authorizes access is stored in a permission-restricted file on that machine and is not transmitted anywhere except to Intuit for token renewal.
  • Intuit. The Application communicates with Intuit's QuickBooks Online API under Intuit's own terms and privacy policy.
  • Anthropic. Accounting data retrieved by the Application is passed to Anthropic's Claude AI service for analysis and categorization at the direction of a company owner, and is handled under Anthropic's applicable terms and privacy policy.

No other third party receives this data.

6. Credentials

The Application authenticates through Intuit's OAuth 2.0 flow. It never receives, requests, or stores any QuickBooks or Intuit account password.

7. Retention and revocation

Authorization can be revoked at any time from the QuickBooks Online company settings (Apps → Disconnect), or by deleting the stored token from the local machine. Revoking access immediately ends the Application's ability to read company data. Intuit refresh tokens also expire automatically after 100 days without use.

8. Security

Access is limited to the owners of Burlak Trucking Inc. Credentials are stored in restricted-permission files on company-controlled hardware. Because the Application is not a public service, it exposes no login page, no public endpoint, and no user database.

9. Children's privacy

The Application is a business accounting tool and is not directed to or usable by children under 13.
