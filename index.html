Burlak Trucking QuickBooks Integration

A private internal accounting tool · Burlak Trucking Inc.

This is a private, internal application used by Burlak Trucking Inc. (USDOT 3277757, MC 1329831) to read its own QuickBooks Online accounting records for bookkeeping, expense categorization, financial review, and tax preparation.

It is not a public product. It is not distributed, sold, or made available to any other business or individual, and it has no public users or account registration. Access is limited to the owners of the company and is authorized through Intuit's OAuth 2.0 flow. The application is configured for read-only access to QuickBooks Online.
